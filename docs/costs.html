Creators are 256× cheaper than Meta US, and 17× cheaper than the cheapest Meta market

I said earlier that Meta should be your volume engine, following your own Boom Match research. On these numbers that's wrong for reach. Use creators to buy attention and Meta only for what creators can't do: precise targeting, retargeting people who played but didn't sign up, and always-on delivery between creator waves.

Budget Meta as a $1–5k/month targeting tool, not as the thing that buys the audience.

How to land at the low end
  • Build tier 0 only first — 3 dev-weeks, not 12. Wave 1 doesn't need the mobile app.
  • Creators for reach; cap Meta at $1k/month.
  • R2 or Bunny, never CloudFront.
  • Affiliates cost nothing until they earn.

That path is roughly $12–15k to find out whether any of this works.

What's missing and why

The prize pool. Daily cash prizes are already live in your product and I have no idea what you're paying out. It could be the largest line here or a rounding error — and unlike everything above, it scales directly with success.

Also excluded: your own time, App Store fees, payment processing on credits, and any legal review — the App Store 5.1.3 read and the camera-roll privacy check both need one.
