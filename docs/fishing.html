the insight first

Two of these ponds should never see a desktop ask

I've been modelling one funnel and getting bad cross-device numbers. That's because I was pushing everyone through the same pipe.

Someone who clicked an ad about losing a client recording already wants a recorder on their work machine. For them, "get it on desktop" isn't a chore you have to bribe them through — it's the thing they came for. The 8% click-to-install rate doesn't apply.

Someone who clicked a memory game does not want your software. Pushing them to a desktop install is where the funnel dies. Don't. Take their $5–20 on credits, on the phone, where they already are.

So the rule is

Recorder-intent traffic → desktop is the destination. Say it immediately and clearly.

Game traffic → the phone is the destination. Never mention desktop in the first session. Monetise on credits, and let desktop happen later, unpaid, if at all.

Mixing them is what produced the $22 CAC. Separated, one pond is cheap and the other doesn't need the step.

What NOT to say in this pond

No "download our desktop app." No "screen recorder." No mention of work. Every one of those is where the funnel leaked $22 a head.

The desktop mention comes on day 7+, by WhatsApp, and only to people who came back and played twice. By then they're a user, not a click.

And when you do say it, say it as money
You've got 3 prize entries waiting.
Claim them on your computer: [link]

"Install our software" converts at 8%. "Collect your winnings" converts at something much closer to 45%. Same click, different sentence.

One rule that overrides all of this

Every ad above sends people to a pricing page that currently says 25 videos — the exact number Loom uses and the loudest complaint in the corpus. Pond A's entire ad is "25 videos, then it stops. That's Loom's free plan, not ours" and it is presently untrue.

Fix the cap before a single ad runs, or you're paying to show people your weakest screen.
