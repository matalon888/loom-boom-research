1 — your question, answered

Both. But Loom users are the small half.

Loom users are reachable but tiny. The only route is Atlassian 6003325467980 (1.9M), JIRA (707k), Confluence (149k). That's roughly 2M people, hot, and worth every dollar — but it will never be your volume.

Fresh audiences are 100× bigger. Small business owners 48.1M · Freelancer 34.3M · Puzzle games 288M. None of them are searching for a screen recorder. Most don't know they want one.

So the answer to "pre-search" is: that's not one tier, it's three of the five below — and it's where all the volume lives.

Why post-search barely exists here

"loom alternative" — 1,000 searches/month in the US at $13.35 a click. Even winning it entirely gets you 1,000 visitors. Meanwhile a single TikTok creator post reaches 5.85M.

Search is a rounding error in this category. Everything real happens before anyone types anything.

The seconds

Reels sweet spot is 21–24 seconds. Under 15s doesn't fit a message; over 30s and completion collapses. The hook window in Reels is 1.5–2 seconds — not three. And 85% of Meta video is watched with sound off, so every one of these has to work muted, with the words on screen.

The 22-second structure

0:00–0:02 the hook, on screen, no logo — this window decides everything and it's under two seconds in Reels.
0:02–0:12 the failure, shown not described — the upload bar stuck at 33%, the "video not found" screen.
0:12–0:18 the same job done in Boom, one take.
0:18–0:22 price against Loom, and the CTA.

Captions burned in throughout. 85% will never hear it.
